Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 23, 2026

Your Privacy Matters: This Privacy Policy explains how FasTapMining collects, uses, and protects your personal information in compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information from Telegram

When you use FasTapMining through Telegram, we receive:

  • User ID: Your unique Telegram user identifier
  • Username: Your Telegram username (if public)
  • Language Preference: Your language setting

1.2 Mining Activity Data

  • Number of taps/mining attempts
  • Hash computations and mining shares
  • Blocks discovered and rewards earned
  • Selected mining pools
  • Token balances (MineX, tBTC, MRDN)
  • NFT rewards collected

1.3 Wallet Information

  • TON wallet addresses (only when you provide them for withdrawals)
  • Transaction history related to reward claims

1.4 Technical Data

  • IP address (for security purposes)
  • Device type and browser information
  • App usage statistics
  • Error logs and crash reports

1.5 Payment Information

Payments are processed through Telegram Stars. We do not store credit card information. Payment data is handled by Telegram according to their privacy policy.

3.2 We Do NOT:

  • ❌ Sell your personal information to third parties
  • ❌ Share your data with advertisers
  • ❌ Use your data for marketing without consent

3.3 Legal Disclosures

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

4. Data Retention

  • Active Users: Data retained while you use the app
  • Inactive Users: Data deleted after 12 months of inactivity

  • Logs: Technical logs retained for 90 days

5. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

5.1 European Users (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw Consent: At any time, where processing is based on consent

5.2 California Users (CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected
  • Know if personal information is sold or disclosed
  • Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

5.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

Contact us via:

  • Bot command: /paysupport
  • Email: privacy@fastapmining.app (if available)

We will respond within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA).

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Secure serverless infrastructure
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security audits
  • Incident response procedures

However: No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your jurisdiction. We ensure adequate safeguards are in place:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for EU data
  • Privacy Shield principles (where applicable)
  • Encryption in transit and at rest

8. Children's Privacy

Age Requirement: FasTapMining is not intended for users under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we discover we have collected data from a child, we will delete it promptly.
