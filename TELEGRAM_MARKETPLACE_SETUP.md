### 2.3 Prepare Documentation for Review

Telegram may ask for:

1. **Privacy Policy URL** (create and host this):
   - Explain what data you collect (Telegram ID, username, wallet addresses)
   - How data is stored (encrypted database)
   - User rights (data deletion on request)

2. **Terms of Service URL**:
   - Mining rules and fair use policy
   - Payment terms (lifetime access)
   - Referral program rules
   - Platform fee disclosure (5% on small tokens)

3. **Contact Email**:
   - Your support email for user issues

**Create these documents and host them at:**
- `https://fas-tap-mining.vercel.app/privacy`
- `https://fas-tap-mining.vercel.app/terms`
- `https://fas-tap-mining.vercel.app/contact`
